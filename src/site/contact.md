Write to [receipt@sus.bot](mailto:receipt@sus.bot). It is read by the people who build and run sus.bot, and it is the right address for anything: a question, a mistake in a report, a licence, an invoice, or a translation that reads badly in your language.

sus.bot is run by [Sitefig](https://sitefig.eu/), a company registered in Luxembourg.

Two things have a faster answer than an email. If `susbot` turned up in your server log, [the crawler page](/bot/) says what it fetched and how to block it. If your question is about what happens to the address you check, [the privacy page](/privacy/) answers it.
